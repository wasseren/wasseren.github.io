How we work

One coordinated pathway from project identification to development readiness.

The process is designed for partners who need Australian market information, factual project analysis and coordinated technical and professional-adviser inputs before determining their next steps.

Step 01

Identify

Identify relevant projects and technology opportunities using client-defined objective criteria across storage, renewable power, industrial decarbonisation and next-generation energy technologies.

Step 02

Screen

Screen projects against client-defined objective criteria, including location, capacity, land status, development approvals, grid-connection progress, project timetable and information supplied by the project proponent.

Step 03

Plan

Define the project engagement scope, development workstreams, adviser responsibilities and coordination pathway for proposed commercial or development collaborations.

Step 04

Coordinate

Coordinate project proponents, technical advisers, independent legal advisers and delivery partners to support project assessment, development planning and implementation.
